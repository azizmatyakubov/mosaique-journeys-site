Destination

Tashkent

Uzbekistan's capital — the green gateway where most Silk Road journeys begin and end.

Where Modern Life Meets Timeless Tradition

Every great journey through Uzbekistan begins in Tashkent. At first glance, the capital may seem different from the ancient Silk Road cities that follow. Wide boulevards replace winding alleyways, contemporary cafés stand beside centuries-old mosques, and modern architecture rises alongside historic landmarks. But spend a little time here, and you'll discover that Tashkent is much more than a gateway — it's a destination in its own right.

As the largest city in Central Asia, Tashkent is constantly evolving while remaining deeply connected to its past. The city's story has been shaped by empires, earthquakes, and generations of people who rebuilt it with remarkable resilience. Today, it is a vibrant blend of cultures, where tradition and innovation exist side by side.

Start your morning with the aroma of freshly baked non at Chorsu Bazaar, one of the oldest and liveliest markets in the country. Wander through stalls overflowing with fragrant spices, dried fruits, handmade sweets, and colourful textiles while locals greet one another with warm smiles and lively conversation. A short walk away, quiet courtyards and historic mosques remind visitors that beneath the modern skyline lies a city with centuries of history. Museums, art galleries, and theatres celebrate Uzbekistan's rich cultural heritage while contemporary artists and designers give the capital a fresh creative spirit.

As the day continues, Tashkent reveals another side of itself. Tree-lined parks invite families to gather, stylish restaurants reinterpret traditional Uzbek cuisine, and rooftop terraces offer views of a city that never stops growing. The capital's famous metro stations — each uniquely decorated with marble, mosaics, and intricate chandeliers — turn an ordinary journey into an unexpected architectural experience.

When evening arrives, Tashkent comes alive in its own rhythm. Locals stroll through illuminated parks, cafés fill with conversation, and the city takes on an atmosphere that is both relaxed and cosmopolitan. It is here that visitors begin to understand modern Uzbekistan — not only through its monuments, but through the people who call this city home.

At Mosaïque Journeys, we see Tashkent as more than the starting point of an itinerary. We encourage our travellers to linger a little longer, explore beyond the famous landmarks, discover independent galleries, enjoy a leisurely dinner beneath the trees, and experience the warmth and openness that define the city's character.

To understand Uzbekistan, you must first understand its heartbeat. And that heartbeat begins in Tashkent.
