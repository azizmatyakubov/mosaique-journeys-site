About Mosaique Journeys

Founded in Uzbekistan, created to share it with the world

Mosaique Journeys began with one simple dream: to help the world discover Uzbekistan through the eyes of someone who proudly calls it home. It was founded by Mehrojbonu Ochilova, an Uzbek woman passionate about travel, cultural exchange, and hospitality.

With a Master's degree in International Business Management and experience in embassy and cultural cooperation, multilingual communication, translation, and tour organization, Mehrojbonu brings a deeply local and international perspective. She is an alumna of CAYLA and CIFUN 2023 and a member of Lions Clubs International.

Today, Mosaique collaborates with trusted professional guides, experienced drivers, and local experts throughout Uzbekistan. With gratitude for the family and friends who helped turn the dream into reality, our purpose remains the same: genuine hospitality, responsible tourism, and journeys that help preserve the country's natural and cultural heritage.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is Uzbekistan safe?

Yes. Uzbekistan is considered one of the world's safest travel destinations. According to the Global Peace Index 2025, it ranks 60th out of 163 countries, placing it among the top 40% most peaceful nations worldwide. In Numbeo's 2026 Safety Index, Uzbekistan ranks 23rd globally with a Safety Index of 73.8, reflecting a high level of safety reported by residents and visitors.

Do I need a visa?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Many countries can enter Uzbekistan visa-free for a certain period, while others are eligible for an e-visa. We will gladly advise you on the entry requirements for your passport and help with the necessary information before your trip.

When is the best time to visit Uzbekistan?

Uzbekistan is a year-round destination, and every season offers a different experience. Spring (March–May): mild temperatures, blooming landscapes, and ideal sightseeing. Summer (June–August): hot and sunny, with long days. Autumn (September–November): pleasant temperatures, colourful scenery, and harvest season. Winter (December–February): fewer tourists, a festive atmosphere, and nearby mountain resorts.

Can I customise every itinerary?

Absolutely. Every journey we create is tailor-made. Destinations, travel pace, hotel category, activities, transport, and cultural experiences can all be adjusted to your interests, schedule, and budget.

Why choose a tailor-made journey instead of a standard tour?

Unlike fixed group tours, our journeys are designed exclusively for you, built around your interests, preferred pace, flight schedule, accommodation style, and budget. This lets you experience Uzbekistan in a more personal, flexible, and meaningful way, without following a rigid group schedule.

What is the weather like?

Uzbekistan has a continental climate with hot summers and cool winters. Spring: 15–30°C (59–86°F). Summer: 30–46°C (86–108°F). Autumn: 15–30°C (59–86°F). Winter: -5–10°C (23–50°F), depending on the region. Weather varies between desert, valleys, and mountains, so we provide personalised packing recommendations before departure.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing and walking shoes are recommended, as many historic sites are explored on foot. In warmer months, lightweight breathable clothing is ideal, with a light jacket for cooler evenings in spring and autumn; in winter, warm layers and a coat. There is no strict dress code for tourists, though modest clothing is appreciated at religious sites, covering shoulders and knees is generally sufficient.

Are solo travellers welcome?

Of course. We design personalised journeys for solo travellers, couples, families, and private groups. Whether you seek cultural discovery, photography, gastronomy, or adventure, we will create an itinerary that matches your travel style.

Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, and other dietary preferences or allergies can usually be accommodated. Please let us know your requirements in advance so we can make the necessary arrangements throughout your journey.

What languages do your guides speak?

Our guides are fluent in English, and guides speaking other languages, including French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Turkish, and more: can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

What currency is used in Uzbekistan?

The official currency is the Uzbekistani Sum (UZS). Major currencies such as Euros (EUR), US Dollars (USD), and British Pounds (GBP) can be exchanged easily at banks, hotels, and official exchange offices. International Visa and Mastercard are accepted in many hotels, restaurants, and larger shops, while carrying some cash is recommended for local markets and smaller businesses.

Can I use credit cards?

Yes. Credit and debit cards are widely accepted in major cities, hotels, and many restaurants. We still recommend carrying some cash when visiting smaller towns, bazaars, or rural areas where card payments may not always be available.

How do we travel between cities?

Uzbekistan has an excellent transport network. Depending on your itinerary, travel may include the modern high-speed train, domestic flights, or comfortable private vehicles with professional drivers. We select the most efficient and comfortable option for each part of your journey.

Is airport transfer included?

Yes. All our tailor-made journeys include airport, railway station, and hotel transfers, ensuring a smooth, stress-free experience from arrival to departure.
